# Fundly AI Scorecard Upgrade

Recommendation History now measures both Fundly's recommendation quality and the investor's decision quality.

## Tracked outcomes

- Every recommendation is measured whether it was followed, ignored, watched or left awaiting a decision.
- Followed recommendations contribute to followed success and direction-adjusted recommendation value.
- Ignored positive recommendations are classified as either missed opportunities or avoided losses.
- Ignored reduce/sell/avoid recommendations are classified as either missed protection or correctly ignored warnings.
- Investor Hub now includes ignored-decision quality alongside Fundly accuracy and followed success.

## Evidence safeguards

The scorecard displays an early-evidence warning until at least 30 recommendations have measured outcomes. Any external performance claim should disclose sample size, measurement horizon and whether ignored recommendations are included.
