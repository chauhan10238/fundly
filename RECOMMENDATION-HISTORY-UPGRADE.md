# Recommendation History Upgrade

- Keeps the feature name **Recommendation History**.
- Automatically measures recommendation outcomes after 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months and 12 months.
- Uses the latest quote available when the user first opens Recommendation History after a horizon becomes due.
- Shows sources, confidence contributors, execution status and investor notes.
- Recommendation Performance remains in Investor Hub and begins populating once at least one recommendation has a measured outcome.

## Important

This is decision-support analytics, not personal financial advice. Outcome measurement is based on the first available quote after each horizon, not necessarily the exact historical closing price at the precise horizon timestamp.
